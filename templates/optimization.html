💡 How Bus Allocation & Frequency Work:
  • Single-Trip ML Forecast: The model predicts passenger count per trip based on corridor attributes.
  • Corridor Frequency: High-density corridors (e.g. Hyderabad–Vijayawada, every 30 mins) handle multiple daily departures.
  • Why 1 vs 2 Buses? When single bus capacity (52 seats) is exceeded by corridor passenger load, a 2nd or 3rd bus is allocated to maintain scheduled frequency and prevent overcrowding.

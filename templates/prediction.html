📖 Demand Level Reference Matrix

HIGH DEMAND

Occupancy ≥ 80% · Severe passenger overcrowding risk. Additional buses must be deployed immediately.

MEDIUM DEMAND

Occupancy 50%–79% · Balanced transit flow and profitable passenger load. Standard frequency maintained.

LOW DEMAND

Occupancy < 50% · Excess seating capacity. Consider optimizing frequency or rerouting buses to busy corridors.
